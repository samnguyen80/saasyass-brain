# Shared language

Use these terms. Do not paraphrase them into longer sentences.

**Demo funnel (six levels)** — Traffic → landing page → qualification → booking → pre-call (call in 2 minutes, send proof) → sales + feedback loop. One button is not a funnel.

**Free-trial funnel (six levels)** — Traffic → landing page → sign-up → in-app onboarding → onboarding emails → conversion. Everything exists to reach the aha moment.

**Aha moment** — The point where the product visibly solves the user's problem. Activation = reaching it; conversion emails start after.

**Creative as targeting** — Under Andromeda, the ad itself (message, person, look) decides who sees it. Different buyers and angles = genuinely different ads, not headline swaps.

**Account map** — One campaign, broad targeting, 20–30 genuinely different ads, at least about $150/day per ad set; scale 10–30% every 2–3 days; refresh by duplicating the ad set.

**Fatigue refresh 70/20/10** — 30 new ads: 70% close remakes of the winner, 20% same message new style, 10% brand-new ideas.

**ABO / CBO** — Budget on each ad set (control) vs on the campaign (efficiency). Low conversion volume makes CBO chase clicks. Test in ABO, scale winners in CBO.

**Swarm strategy** — Attention + teaching + closing ads in the same campaign so one buyer meets all three.

**Signal quality** — What Meta learns from. Send booked and rep-qualified events via the Conversions API; aim for match quality 8+; always include phone.

**Learning limited** — Too few conversions per ad set (about 50/week is the claimed need) so Meta wanders to colder audiences.

**Existing-customer leak** — Best ad mostly reaching people already in your base or pipeline. Check frequency, new vs existing split, attribution window; fix with 7-day click + exclusions + CRM judging.

**Max cost per call** — Most you can pay for one booked call and still hit payback. Compare to actual cost per call before pausing ads.

**Payback** — Months of gross profit to earn back CAC.

**Hammer-them strategy** — Ads shown only to people who just booked, 15–20 content pieces before the call, kept going through the deal.

**VSL** — Video sales letter on the landing page that does the explaining so reps only close. Five parts: summary, problem, new way (demo), proof, offer.

**Market vote** — Test each positioning idea as selfie video + static ad + same-headline landing page in its own ad set (~$50/day, 5–7 days); judge by qualified leads in the CRM.

**Cancellation flow** — Feedback email on cancel → save vs can't-save split → incentive or thank-you → win-back at 90 days; returners go back through onboarding.

**Churn flows (three)** — No-login (2x the normal gap), low-usage (off the key-feature path), cancellation-page-visits (answer top 3 objections, never "we saw you").

**Claimed** — Any Saasyass figure. Not audited here.
