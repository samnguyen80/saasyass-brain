# Playbook — B2B SaaS acquisition → activation → retention

The corpus is 19 reels from one creator, @saasyass, on Meta-driven B2B SaaS growth. Figures are **claimed**. Each stage points to the concept that owns the detail.

## 0. Know your numbers first

[[Unit economics calculator]]: full CAC, gross-profit LTV, payback, and the max cost per booked call. Decide whether a cost per demo is high *before* pausing ads.

## 1. Pick what to say

[[Market vote positioning]]: test offers and angles as selfie + static + same-headline page, ABO, ~$50/day each, judged on CRM-qualified leads.

## 2. Build the right ads

- [[Creative as targeting]]: personas x angles, genuinely different ads.
- [[Swarm strategy]]: attention + teaching + closing in one campaign.
- [[SaaS ad tier list]]: founder video and static reels first; fast-to-make formats win.

## 3. Structure the account

[[Meta account structure]] (one campaign, broad, 20-30 ads, ~$150/day/ad set, 10-30% raises, 70/20/10 refresh) and [[ABO vs CBO]] (test in ABO, scale in CBO).

## 4. Give Meta clean signal

[[Conversion signal quality]]: Conversions API for bookings and rep-qualified events, phone included, match 8+, enough conversions per ad set. Then audit for the leak in [[Existing customer exclusion]].

## 5. Convert: two funnel shapes

- Sales-led: [[Demo funnel six levels]] with [[Video sales letter]] and [[Hammer them retargeting]].
- Self-serve: [[Trial onboarding flow]] (activation first, conversion second).

## 6. Retain

[[Churn win-back flows]] (no-login, low-usage, cancel-page) and [[Cancellation flow]] (feedback, save split, 90-day win-back, re-onboard).

## 7. Close the loop

Sales-call recordings and cancellation feedback become the next batch of ad angles ([[Demo funnel six levels]] level 6, [[Cancellation flow]]).
