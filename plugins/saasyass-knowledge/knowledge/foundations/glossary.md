# Glossary

Canonical terms. See also repo `CONTEXT.md`.

## Economics

**CAC** — Total cost to win a customer, including sales pay and tools. [[Unit economics calculator]]

**LTV** — Customer lifetime value in gross profit.

**Payback** — Months of gross profit to recover CAC.

**Max cost per call** — Ceiling per booked call that still hits the payback target.

## Meta

**Creative as targeting** — The ad decides who sees it. [[Creative as targeting]]

**ABO / CBO** — Budget per ad set vs per campaign. [[ABO vs CBO]]

**Account map** — One campaign, broad, 20-30 different ads. [[Meta account structure]]

**70/20/10 refresh** — Remakes / new style / new ideas. [[Meta account structure]]

**Swarm strategy** — Attention + teaching + closing together. [[Swarm strategy]]

**Conversions API** — Server-side events to Meta. [[Conversion signal quality]]

**Match quality** — Meta's 0-10 event match score; target 8+ (*claimed*).

**Learning limited** — Too few conversions per ad set. [[Conversion signal quality]]

**Existing-customer leak** — Ad credited for conversions among people already in the base. [[Existing customer exclusion]]

**Hammer-them** — Ads only to booked prospects. [[Hammer them retargeting]]

**Market vote** — Paid test of positioning ideas. [[Market vote positioning]]

## Funnel and retention

**Demo funnel** — Six levels from traffic to feedback. [[Demo funnel six levels]]

**VSL** — Video sales letter, five parts. [[Video sales letter]]

**Aha moment** — Product visibly solves the user's problem. [[Trial onboarding flow]]

**Activation** — Reaching the aha moment.

**Cancellation flow** — [[Cancellation flow]]

**No-login / low-usage / cancel-page flows** — [[Churn win-back flows]]
