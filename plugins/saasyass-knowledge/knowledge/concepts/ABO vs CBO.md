# ABO vs CBO

Watch: [ABO vs CBO](https://www.instagram.com/reel/Dd10T5Qvqku/)

Budget placement is a choice between control and efficiency.

- Hierarchy: campaign (goal) → ad sets (audience and optimisation) → ads (creative).
- **ABO** puts the budget on each ad set. Every ad set spends, so new ads get a fair shot and you can grow several winners at once.
- **CBO** puts one budget on the campaign. Meta routes it to apparent winners: efficient, but it concentrates spend and leaves no tested bench when the winner tires.
- B2B SaaS catch: with few conversions, CBO drifts toward whatever gets clicks, usually the meme with weak leads. See [[Conversion signal quality]].

## Rule of thumb

- Spending under a few hundred dollars a day: a single ad set, so the choice barely matters.
- Spending more: test new ads in ABO, move proven winners to CBO.
- Positioning tests also use ABO, one ad set per idea: [[Market vote positioning]].
