# Video sales letter

Watch: [VSL For B2B SaaS](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DeFq8XjuBp2/)

Record the education once; let reps close.

Why: reps otherwise re-explain every demo, poor fits do not filter themselves, and the contact cannot forward a pitch to a boss.

Five parts:

1. **Summary** (first 60-90 seconds): who it's for, problem, result, next step.
2. **Problem**: monthly cost and why usual fixes (hire, buy another tool) fail.
3. **New way**: show approach, then product doing the one job the buyer cares most about, with realistic data and before/after. Not a feature tour.
4. **Proof**: real customers like the viewer.
5. **Offer**: what they get, risk reducer (e.g. pilot), what the call involves, one booking step.

Page: video under a clear headline, booking form, case studies, FAQ; chapters on the video.
