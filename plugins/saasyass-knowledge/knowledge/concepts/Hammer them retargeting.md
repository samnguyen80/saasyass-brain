# Hammer them retargeting

Watch: [Hammer Them Strategy](https://www.instagram.com/reel/Dd4pquRhVim/)

Close the gap between booking and the call with ads shown only to booked prospects.

- Audience: pixel on the booking-confirmation page, recent visitors only. Tiny, cheap.
- Target 15-20 content pieces seen before the call (*claimed*): founder phone videos answering rep FAQs (onboarding, pricing, versus the alternative, a customer story) plus some long formats.
- Keep going after the call. Long cycles with several signers mean ads continue until sign or no, with pieces for blockers (security explainer for IT, cost breakdown for finance).
- This is the sanctioned exception to [[Existing customer exclusion]]: segregated audience, not cold prospecting.
