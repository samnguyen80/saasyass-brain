# SaaS ad tier list

Watch: [SaaS Ad Tier List](https://www.instagram.com/reel/Dd9ypDjTS2L/)

Format ranking for B2B SaaS paid social (the creator's opinion, *claimed*; he invites disagreement).

| Tier | Formats |
|---|---|
| S | founder to camera (30-60s, phone, captions, quick cuts); static reels (background footage plus changing bold text) |
| A | static images; carousels; AI-made ads |
| B | testimonials (a well-captioned story can reach S); organic posts as ads; paid creators |
| C | memes; long webinar or podcast recordings (only the best 2-5 minutes); polished brand video |
| F | anything without captions or editing |

Logic: most ads fail and few win, so formats you can produce in volume, fast and cheap, win over time. Images and memes can pull leads or clicks that know little about you or will never buy, especially in a demo funnel.
