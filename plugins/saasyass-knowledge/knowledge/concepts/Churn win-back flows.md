# Churn win-back flows

Watch: [Churn Email Flows](https://www.instagram.com/reel/Dd_yjwMTr3u/)

Three behaviour-triggered flows that catch churn early.

1. **No-login**: find the longest normal gap between logins for an active user, double it; past that, send results reminder, current customer wins, and a same-industry answer to the usual excuse.
2. **Low-usage**: map key features, frequency and best pairings; email as soon as a user drifts off that path (skips a key feature, uses too rarely, relies on one thing).
3. **Cancellation-page visits**: repeated visits in a week signal intent; do not mention you saw them. Answer the top three cancel reasons in a friendly FAQ-style email.

All depend on emails reacting to in-product behaviour, not calendar blasts. After cancel, move to [[Cancellation flow]]. Illustrative churn math in the reel (about 6% monthly, ~half gone in a year) is *claimed*. Template pack promised via comment only: [[Comment-gated resources]].
