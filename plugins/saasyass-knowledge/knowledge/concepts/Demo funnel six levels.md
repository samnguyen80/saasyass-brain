# Demo funnel six levels

Watch: [Six-Level Demo Funnel](https://www.instagram.com/reel/Dd9lDnFh0Va/)

A booking button is not a funnel. Six levels:

1. **Traffic**: static and video ads to a dedicated offer, not the homepage.
2. **Landing page**: clear promise, short problem/solution video ([[Video sales letter]]), one book-call button, no menu, blog or careers.
3. **Qualification**: company size, industry, problem; unqualified cannot book; report only qualified to Meta ([[Conversion signal quality]]).
4. **Booking** straight onto the calendar.
5. **Pre-call**, where most demos die: call within two minutes of booking (*claimed* target), confirm, send case studies and offer; add [[Hammer them retargeting]] ads.
6. **Sales and feedback**: track shows, qualified, closed, revenue; mine call recordings with AI for buyer wording and objections to seed new ads.
