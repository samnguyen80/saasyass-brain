# Cancellation flow

Watch: [Cancellation Flow](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DeAoqlesKAi/)

Cancellation is feedback from paying customers.

1. On cancel, one email with a short feedback form: why, what frustrated, what would have changed their mind, would a discount or more time keep them.
2. After ~20 answers, read the pattern (onboarding, missing feature, pricing fit); that is the bottleneck.
3. A day later, split. **Unsavable**: thank-you, no discount, no guilt. **Savable**: thank-you with a real incentive and one-click reactivate, then a second email on what they lose.
4. Both go to a win-back flow ~90 days later.
5. Returners re-enter onboarding ([[Trial onboarding flow]]); otherwise the cause repeats.
