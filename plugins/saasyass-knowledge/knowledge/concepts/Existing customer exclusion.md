# Existing customer exclusion

Watch: [Best Ad Shows To Existing Customers](https://www.instagram.com/reel/Dd9umybMRB4/)

A best-ever ad may mostly be reaching people who already pay or are mid-pipeline.

Three checks:

1. **Frequency.** Rising means one small group sees it repeatedly.
2. **New vs existing split.** Look for spend on current customers, trial users and open-pipeline leads.
3. **Attribution.** If view-only exposure counts, an ad can be credited for a booking caused by a rep's email.

Consequences: Meta funds the flattering ad, truly good ads never get budget, teams clone a non-winner, and scaling raises cost while new pipeline stays flat.

Fix: optimise on 7-day click; upload customer and pipeline lists as exclusions on cold campaigns; judge each ad on new qualified pipeline in the CRM, not the platform number.
