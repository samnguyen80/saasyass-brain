# Swarm strategy

Watch: [Swarm Strategy](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DdwmZBqtSje/)

Run all three ad jobs in one campaign instead of testing one type at a time.

| Buyer moment | Ad job | Examples named |
|---|---|---|
| Feels the pain, no fix in mind | Attention | meme, skit, bold offer |
| Knows the fix, choosing who | Teaching | founder at whiteboard, loom walkthrough |
| Ready, comparing vendors | Closing | paid pilot with written results, versus hiring, versus in-house build |

- Single-type tests mislead: funny ads get views, educational get saves, offers feel salesy, and each looks like a failure because it was asked to do the whole job.
- Putting all three in one campaign lets the same buyer meet them in sequence across a week.
