# Unit economics calculator

Watch: [CAC LTV Payback Calculator](https://www.instagram.com/reel/Dd6rG7XNPsz/) · [Max Cost Per Call Calculator](https://www.instagram.com/reel/Dd4KZYoBT_S/)

Know the numbers before you judge ad cost.

- **CAC** counts everything: sales pay, commissions, tools, agency, not only ad spend.
- **LTV** uses gross profit, not revenue.
- **LTV:CAC** vs a roughly 3:1 board norm; **payback** (months of gross profit to recover CAC) decides whether runway can fund it.
- **Max cost per call**: from booked → show → qualified → close rates, derive booked calls per customer; allowable acquisition spend per customer (margin-adjusted for your payback target) divided by that gives the ceiling per booked call. Below is a bargain, above a donation.

Worked numbers in the reels (*claimed*, dramatised): $80k all-in for four customers gives $20k CAC; $24k/yr at 80% margin over four years gives ~$77k LTV, ~4:1, ~12.5 months payback. Separate example: 70% show, 60% qualified, 25% close, $2,000/month contract gives ~$17k allowable spend and ~$1,700 ceiling vs $400 paid.
