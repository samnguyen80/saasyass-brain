# Trial onboarding flow

Watch: [Free Trial Six-Level Funnel](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DeIP5F7MjzY/) · [Trial Onboarding Emails](https://www.instagram.com/reel/Dd65gT-BclW/) · [Onboarding Email Flow Doc](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DXFP1_pDPD_/)

Self-serve equivalent of the demo funnel: every level exists to reach the aha moment.

## Six levels

1. Traffic naming the buyer's situation; cold goes to a dedicated page.
2. Landing page: one promise, short aha-moment video, proof, one start-trial button.
3. Sign-up: social login, few fields, one goal question; report **activated** users to Meta ([[Conversion signal quality]]).
4. In-app onboarding: tied welcome, three-step checklist with progress, templates or sample data, optional tour with contextual tips.
5. Behaviour-triggered emails for the same three steps.
6. Conversion: upgrade prompts after value moments, pre-expiry sequence, sales touch for heavy high-value usage, no-activity then win-back.

## Email mechanics

- Two sets: **activation** (to the aha moment) then **conversion** (offers/incentives), the second only after the first.
- Define three value steps; one email per branch (did A, missed B → B email, then a how-to follow-up).
- Email shape: one headline, progress bar, short how-to video, brief text, one job.
- After-trial flow for those who never reached the aha moment.
- Claimed results (*claimed*): +8.5% trial conversion, some emails ~21%.
