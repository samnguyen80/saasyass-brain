# Creative as targeting

Watch: [Andromeda Creative Targeting](https://www.instagram.com/reel/Dd1YQq4yDNJ/)

Detailed audience stacks matter less; the ad now carries the targeting.

- Meta's system (named Andromeda in the reel) reads message, visuals and who is on screen to decide who sees the ad.
- Work in buyer personas, because B2B buying is a group: finance, sales leadership, the CEO, operations. One ad "for everyone" lands on nobody.
- For each persona choose angles: the pain felt now, the result wanted, the alternative being compared (hire, build in-house).
- Four personas x three angles = twelve ads. A new headline over the same video is one ad, not twelve; different means a different person, message and look.
- Winning ads for a persona pull more of that persona. This is why [[Meta account structure]] asks for 20-30 different ads.
