# Market vote positioning

Watch: [Let The Market Vote](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DdzjJzBv9J7/)

Settle positioning disputes with a paid test before building a website.

1. List every competing offer or angle (e.g. audit, benchmark, paid pilot; or ICP-led vs consolidation messaging).
2. For each, make a selfie video and a static ad with the idea as headline, plus an ugly landing page with the same headline and a form.
3. One campaign, one ad set per idea with its own budget ([[ABO vs CBO]]) so no idea is pre-judged.
4. About $50/day per idea for 5-7 days (*claimed*).
5. Judge by cost per lead and by whether leads qualify in the CRM; a cheap student lead is the worst buy.
6. The winner becomes positioning for site, ads, deck and sales calls.

This is a test design, not the steady-state budget; for that see [[Meta account structure]].
