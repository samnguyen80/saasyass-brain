# Six-Level Demo Funnel

- **Watch:** https://www.instagram.com/reel/Dd9lDnFh0Va/
- **Date:** 2026-10-01
- **Playbook:** [[playbook]]

**Thesis:** A B2B SaaS demo funnel has six levels, and the biggest leak is between booking and the call.

## Doctrine

- Level 1 traffic: static and video ads to a dedicated offer, not the homepage.
- Level 2 landing page: clear promise, short video on problem and solution, one book-call button; no menu, blog or careers.
- Level 3 qualification: questions on company size, industry, problem before the calendar; unqualified people cannot book, and only qualified ones are reported back to Meta.
- Level 4 booking straight onto the calendar, no email back-and-forth.
- Level 5 pre-call, where most demos die: call within two minutes of booking, confirm attendance, send case studies and the offer ahead.
- Level 6 sales and feedback: track show, qualified, closed and revenue; run call recordings through AI to extract buyer words and objections, and turn them into the next ads' angles.

## Numbers

- Two-minute speed-to-call target (*claimed* rule).
