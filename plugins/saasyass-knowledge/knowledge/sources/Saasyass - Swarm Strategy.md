# Swarm Strategy

- **Watch:** https://www.instagram.com/reel/DdwmZBqtSje/
- **Date:** 2026-09-26
- **Playbook:** [[playbook]]

**Thesis:** Run attention, teaching and closing ads together so a buyer meets all three stages of the same campaign.

## Doctrine

- Buyers pass three moments: feel a problem, learn the fix and shop, then compare vendors. A single ad serves only one.
- Attention ads for problem-aware people: memes, skits, bold offers. Teaching ads for solution-aware: founder at a whiteboard, loom. Closing ads for ready buyers: paid pilot with written results, versus hiring, versus building in-house.
- Test failures (funny ads got views, educational got saves, offers felt salesy) were each a good ad doing the whole job alone.
- Same buyer sees a meme, then teaching, then an offer across the week.
