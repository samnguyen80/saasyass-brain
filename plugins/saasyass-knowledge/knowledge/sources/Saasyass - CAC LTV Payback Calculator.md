# CAC LTV Payback Calculator

- **Watch:** https://www.instagram.com/reel/Dd6rG7XNPsz/
- **Date:** 2026-09-30
- **Playbook:** [[playbook]]

**Thesis:** Count CAC fully, value customers in gross profit, then check LTV:CAC and payback against runway.

## Doctrine

- CAC includes sales salaries, commissions, tools and agencies, not only ad spend; ad-only counting understates it badly.
- LTV is gross profit over lifetime, not revenue.
- Ratio LTV:CAC compared to a board norm of roughly 3:1; payback months decide survival even when the ratio is fine.
- Whether a long payback is acceptable depends on cash runway.

## Numbers

- Worked example (*claimed*): $20k ad spend and four customers, $80k all-in spend gives $20k CAC; $24k/yr contract at 80% margin over four years is about $77k LTV, near 4:1; about $1,600/month gross profit means roughly 12.5 months payback.
