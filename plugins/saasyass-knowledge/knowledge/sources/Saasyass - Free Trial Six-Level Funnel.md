# Free Trial Six-Level Funnel

- **Watch:** https://www.instagram.com/reel/DeIP5F7MjzY/
- **Date:** 2026-10-05
- **Playbook:** [[playbook]]

**Thesis:** A free-trial funnel is six levels that all point at one moment, the aha moment; a sign-up button plus a welcome email leaves trial users alone.

## Doctrine

- Level 1 traffic: ads that name the buyer's exact situation; cold traffic lands on a dedicated page, warm people can go straight to sign-up.
- Level 2 landing page: one promise, a short video of the product reaching the aha moment, a little proof, one start-trial button. No menu, blog or careers link.
- Level 3 sign-up: social login, minimal fields, one question about the job they want done, which steers what they see next. Report activated users (not every sign-up) back to Meta.
- Level 4 in-app onboarding is where trials are won or lost: message tied to their goal, three-step checklist with progress bar, templates or sample data so no empty screens. Product tour optional, tips contextual.
- Level 5 triggered onboarding emails mirror the same three steps; conversion emails start only after the aha moment.
- Level 6 conversion: upgrade prompts right after a value moment, a pre-expiry sequence, sales outreach to heavy-usage high-value accounts, then no-activity and later win-back flows.

- Illustrative scenario: 400 sign-ups a month, 11 pay (*claimed* as a dramatised example, not a real account).
