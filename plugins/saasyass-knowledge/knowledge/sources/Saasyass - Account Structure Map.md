# Account Structure Map

- **Watch:** https://www.instagram.com/reel/DeAWutMuVv3/
- **Date:** 2026-10-02
- **Playbook:** [[playbook]]

**Thesis:** Replace panic structure with one campaign, broad targeting, many different ads and patient budgets.

## Doctrine

- Failure modes named: new ads dropped into the best campaign crash it; one campaign per idea at tiny budgets never exits learning; the single winner dies with no bench.
- Fix: one campaign, broad targeting, 20-30 ads that differ in pain point, outcome and on-screen person; Meta uses those differences to find buyers.
- Budget floor per ad set around $150/day; if you can afford only one ad set at that level, run one.
- Judge only after spend roughly equals what one new customer is worth paying for; let good ones run to about double. Early panic kills near-winners.
- Scale 10-30% every two to three days while sales can absorb the calls.
- Fatigue signal: cost per call rises and call count falls for three or four days. Response: duplicate the winning ad set, load 30 new ads (70% close remakes, 20% same message new style, 10% new ideas), pause the tired set.

## Numbers

- $150/day per ad set, 20-30 ads, 10-30% raises every 2-3 days, 70/20/10 refresh (all *claimed* rules of thumb).
