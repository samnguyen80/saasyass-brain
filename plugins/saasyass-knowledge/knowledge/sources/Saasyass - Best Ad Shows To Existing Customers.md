# Best Ad Shows To Existing Customers

- **Watch:** https://www.instagram.com/reel/Dd9umybMRB4/
- **Date:** 2026-10-01
- **Playbook:** [[playbook]]

**Thesis:** A suspiciously good ad may mostly reach people already in your base or pipeline and take credit for conversions that were happening anyway.

## Doctrine

- Check 1, frequency: a climbing number means the same small group keeps seeing it.
- Check 2, new vs existing breakdown: look for budget spent on current customers, trial users and open-pipeline leads.
- Check 3, attribution: the default can credit view-only exposure, so a rep email followed by a booking gets credited to the ad.
- Consequence: Meta over-funds the inflated ad, real winners starve, and teams clone an ad that never worked; scaling then raises cost while pipeline stays flat.
- Fix: optimise on 7-day click, upload customer and pipeline lists as exclusions on cold campaigns, judge every ad on new qualified pipeline in the CRM.
