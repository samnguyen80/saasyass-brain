# Trial Onboarding Emails

- **Watch:** https://www.instagram.com/reel/Dd65gT-BclW/
- **Date:** 2026-09-30
- **Playbook:** [[playbook]]

**Thesis:** Two email sets: first get users to the aha moment, then ask them to pay, and never send the second until the first is done.

## Doctrine

- Users pay when the product has solved their problem; most trial users never get there.
- Define the three actions that deliver real value; send behaviour-triggered emails for each (did step one, missed step two, get the step-two email, then a follow-up showing exactly how).
- Email format: one headline, a short video of the step, a few lines of text, one job per email.
- Only after the aha moment send offer emails (discount or bonus) until upgrade or trial end.

## Numbers

- Claimed: this flow lifted trial conversion by about 8.5 points extra, some emails converting around 21% (*claimed*, unaudited).
