# ABO vs CBO

- **Watch:** https://www.instagram.com/reel/Dd10T5Qvqku/
- **Date:** 2026-09-28
- **Playbook:** [[playbook]]

**Thesis:** ABO vs CBO is control vs efficiency, and low conversion volume pushes CBO toward click-chasing.

## Doctrine

- Hierarchy: campaign (goal), ad sets (who and optimisation), ads (creative). Company analogy: campaign, departments, employees.
- ABO: budget per ad set, each gets money regardless; forces spend into new ads and builds multiple winners.
- CBO: one campaign budget moved by Meta to apparent winners; concentrates spend and starves the rest, so the bench is empty when the winner tires.
- B2B SaaS twist: with few conversions CBO optimises toward cheap clicks, often the meme with poor leads.
- Rule: under a few hundred dollars a day use one ad set; above that test new ads in ABO and scale proven ones in CBO.
