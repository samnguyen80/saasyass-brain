# Hammer Them Strategy

- **Watch:** https://www.instagram.com/reel/Dd4pquRhVim/
- **Date:** 2026-09-29
- **Playbook:** [[playbook]]

**Thesis:** After someone books, show ads only to them so they arrive informed and show up, and keep going through the long B2B deal.

## Doctrine

- Gap: booking to call is usually one confirmation email, so half no-show and the rest ask what you do.
- Audience: put the pixel on the booking confirmation page and build a recent-lander audience; run content only to it, a tiny and cheap audience.
- Goal: 15-20 content pieces seen before the call. Source them cheaply: founder phone videos answering rep FAQs (onboarding, pricing, versus the spreadsheet, a customer story), plus a few long formats.
- Do not stop after the call; with multi-month cycles and many signers, keep ads until sign or no, and add pieces for blockers such as a security video for IT and a cost breakdown for finance.
