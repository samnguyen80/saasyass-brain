# VSL For B2B SaaS

- **Watch:** https://www.instagram.com/reel/DeFq8XjuBp2/
- **Date:** 2026-10-04
- **Playbook:** [[playbook]]

**Thesis:** Record the explanation once as a video sales letter so reps stop narrating the same pitch on every demo.

## Doctrine

- Problem it solves: reps spend a demo explaining basics, then the contact must forward a pitch to a boss and it dies.
- A VSL pre-educates, self-filters poor fits and lets the champion forward the video internally.
- Five parts: summary (first 60-90 seconds cover who, problem, result, next step), problem (monthly cost, why usual fixes fail), new way plus demo of one workflow, proof from lookalike customers, offer with risk reducer such as a pilot and a single booking step.
- Do not tour every feature; show the one job the buyer cares about and keep the full demo for the tailored call.
- Page layout: video under a clear headline, booking form below, then case studies and FAQ; add chapters so viewers can skip.
