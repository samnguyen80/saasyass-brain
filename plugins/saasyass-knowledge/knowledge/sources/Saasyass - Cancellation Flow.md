# Cancellation Flow

- **Watch:** https://www.instagram.com/reel/DeAoqlesKAi/
- **Date:** 2026-10-02
- **Playbook:** [[playbook]]

**Thesis:** Treat every cancellation as paid feedback: ask why, split savable from unsavable, and send returners back through onboarding.

## Doctrine

- Immediately after cancel, send one short feedback form: why, what frustrated them, what would have changed their mind, would a discount or extra time keep them.
- After roughly 20 answers a pattern shows the real bottleneck (confusing onboarding, missing feature, pricing for small teams).
- A day later split by answers. Unsavable: short thank-you, no discount, no guilt, door left open. Savable: thank-you with a real incentive and a one-click reactivate button, then a second email on what they lose.
- Both groups enter a win-back flow about 90 days later, by which time the cause may be fixed.
- Anyone who returns re-enters onboarding, otherwise you are scheduling the next cancellation.

## Lead magnet

- Promised the full flow (emails, form, timing, splits) via comment 'cancellation'. Not received here.
