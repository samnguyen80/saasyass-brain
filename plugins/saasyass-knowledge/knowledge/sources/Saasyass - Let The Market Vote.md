# Let The Market Vote

- **Watch:** https://www.instagram.com/reel/DdzjJzBv9J7/
- **Date:** 2026-09-27
- **Playbook:** [[playbook]]

**Thesis:** Settle positioning arguments with a cheap paid test before building a new website.

## Doctrine

- List every competing offer or angle (audit, benchmark, paid pilot, ICP-led vs consolidation message).
- Per idea build a selfie video, a static ad and a plain landing page with the same headline and a form.
- One campaign, one ad set per idea, own budget (ABO) so none is pre-judged.
- About $50/day per idea for 5-7 days; judge cost per lead and lead quality in the CRM, since a cheap unqualified lead is the costliest.
- Winner becomes positioning across site, ads, deck and sales calls.

## Numbers

- $50/day per idea, 5-7 days (*claimed* test design).
