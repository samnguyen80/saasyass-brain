# Andromeda Creative Targeting

- **Watch:** https://www.instagram.com/reel/Dd1YQq4yDNJ/
- **Date:** 2026-09-28
- **Playbook:** [[playbook]]

**Thesis:** Hand-built audiences are mostly decoration now; Meta reads the ad and the targeting job moved into creative.

## Doctrine

- Under Meta's Andromeda system, the platform reads message, visuals and on-screen people to match an ad to likely responders.
- Your job: know each buyer persona in the buying group (finance, sales lead, CEO, operations) and pick several angles each: current pain, desired result, the alternative they compare you to.
- Four buyers times three angles gives twelve ads; changing only a headline counts as one ad.
- Different means different person, message and look. If the finance ad wins, Meta finds more finance buyers.
