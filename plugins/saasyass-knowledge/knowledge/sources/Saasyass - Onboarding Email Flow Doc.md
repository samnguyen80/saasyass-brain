# Onboarding Email Flow Doc

- **Watch:** https://www.instagram.com/reel/DXFP1_pDPD_/
- **Date:** 2026-04-13
- **Playbook:** [[playbook]]

**Thesis:** The same two-part onboarding flow in long form: activation first, conversion second, then an after-trial safety net.

## Doctrine

- Flow trigger: free-trial sign-up. Part one activation (reach the aha moment), part two conversion that only begins when part one finishes.
- Map the customer journey to three steps that solve the problem they came for; build three emails per step (nine) with branching on what they did.
- Each step email: tie to pain, show how inside the email, video backup, a help route; congratulate then push the next step.
- Good email: big headline, progress bar, a how-to video, text guide beneath; bad email: wall of text.
- Part two: offers, incentives or discounts after the aha moment until upgrade or expiry. Separate after-trial flow for users who never reached it.
- Rationale given: email is a large underused channel and most founders send only transactional mail.
