# SaaS Ad Tier List

- **Watch:** https://www.instagram.com/reel/Dd9ypDjTS2L/
- **Date:** 2026-10-01
- **Playbook:** [[playbook]]

**Thesis:** Rank ad formats by how fast and cheaply you can make many of them, since most ads fail and few become winners.

## Doctrine

- S tier: founder talking to camera (short phone video with captions and fast cuts) and static reels (background footage with changing bold text).
- A tier: static images, carousels (thread-to-slides is underused) and AI-made ads; cheap and stable but demo-funnel leads often arrive knowing less.
- B tier: customer testimonials, organic posts as ads, paid creators; a strong captioned customer story can climb to S, paid actors for serious software sound fake.
- C tier: memes (curious clicks, no buyers), long webinars and podcasts (only if cut to the best two to five minutes), polished brand video.
- F tier: anything with no captions and no editing.
- Logic: make-fast formats win over volume, because winners are rare.
