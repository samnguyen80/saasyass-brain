# Max Cost Per Call Calculator

- **Watch:** https://www.instagram.com/reel/Dd4KZYoBT_S/
- **Date:** 2026-09-29
- **Playbook:** [[playbook]]

**Thesis:** A cost per demo is only expensive relative to a computed max cost per call; compute it before pausing.

## Doctrine

- Walk the funnel: booked to showed to qualified to closed gives booked calls per customer.
- Take the margin-adjusted allowable spend per customer for your payback target, divide by booked calls per customer.
- Anything below the ceiling is a bargain; above is a donation. Compare to the competitor who pays little per call but never closes.
- A calculator takes contract value, show, qualified and close rates, and payback target.

## Numbers

- Example (*claimed*): 70% show, 60% of those qualified, one quarter closed gives about 1 customer per 10 booked calls; $2,000/month contract allows about $17k acquisition spend, so max cost per call about $1,700 versus $400 paid.
