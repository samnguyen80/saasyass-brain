# SaaS Meta ads structure

Read `${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/knowledge/concepts/Meta account structure.md`, then `ABO vs CBO.md`, `Creative as targeting.md`, and `Swarm strategy.md`. Tag figures **claimed**. No shell. Do not publish ads or touch a live account.

## Do

Give a short plan: structure, daily budget per ad set, how many ads and how they differ, when to judge, how to scale, how to refresh. Compare to what they described; name the specific deviation. If their budget is under the floor in the concept, say run one ad set. If asked about ad formats, point to `concepts/SaaS ad tier list.md`. If they ask for the "full account map" file, say it was comment-gated and is not in the brain.

## Completion

They can state the structure, the budget floor and the refresh rule.
